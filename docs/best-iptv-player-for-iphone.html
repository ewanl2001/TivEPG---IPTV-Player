What to look for in an IPTV player

  • A proper TV guide (EPG). A grid you can browse while the channel keeps playing is the difference between "a list of links" and real TV.
  • Xtream Codes and M3U support. Xtream Codes brings the guide, catch-up and VOD automatically. M3U needs an XMLTV guide URL.
  • Native apps on every device. One purchase that works on iPhone, iPad and Apple TV, with layouts made for each.
  • Stable playback. Live streams drop. Good players recover on their own instead of freezing.
  • Catch-up and VOD. If your provider archives channels, you want to be able to watch back.
  • No channels bundled. Legitimate players on the App Store never include streams. If an app promises free channels, avoid it.
