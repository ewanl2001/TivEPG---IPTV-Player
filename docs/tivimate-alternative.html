Is TiviMate available on iOS or Apple TV?

No. TiviMate is made for Android TV, Google TV, Fire TV and Android boxes. There is no official TiviMate app for iPhone, iPad or Apple TV, and App Store apps with similar names are not made by the TiviMate developer.

That leaves a lot of people who switch to an Apple TV or iPhone looking for the same thing: a guide-first IPTV player that feels like real TV. That is what TivEPG was built to be.

Moving from TiviMate to TivEPG

  1. Find your provider's details: an Xtream Codes server, username and password, or an M3U URL and EPG URL. They are the same ones you used in TiviMate.
  2. Install TivEPG from the App Store on your iPhone, iPad or Apple TV.
  3. Open Settings → Playlists → Add Playlist and enter them. On Apple TV, TivEPG Remote lets you do it from your phone.

What about IPTV Smarters, iPlayTV, UHF and others?

They all play IPTV on Apple devices and they are all worth knowing about. TivEPG's focus is different: it is built around the TV guide and live sport, so it tells you which of your channels has the game, shows live stats beside it and keeps scores from spoiling the stream.

Questions

Is there a TiviMate app for iPhone?

No. TiviMate is Android only. TivEPG is a TiviMate-style IPTV player for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV.

Is there TiviMate for Apple TV?

No. TiviMate does not run on tvOS. TivEPG gives Apple TV a full EPG guide, catch-up, Multiview and a Sports Hub.

Can I use my TiviMate playlist in TivEPG?

Yes. Use the same Xtream Codes login or M3U and EPG URLs from your provider.
