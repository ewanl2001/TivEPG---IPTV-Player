Remote Help

Set up TivEPG
for someone else.

Sort out their playlists, channels, movies, series and Sports Hub from here. They don't need to do a thing.

  1. 1
    On their iPhone, iPad or Apple TV

    They open TivEPG, go to Settings and choose Remote Help.

  2. 2
    They tell you the number

    It's 6 digits. If they send you a photo of the square code, scan it and the number fills in by itself.

  3. 3
    You make the changes here

    Everything appears on their screen straight away, and they can see that you're connected.

  • Encrypted between you and their device
  • No accounts, nothing stored
  • Ends when they stop it, or after 30 minutes
