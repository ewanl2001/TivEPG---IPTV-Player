What makes a good IPTV app for iPhone?

Most IPTV players on the App Store are ports of Android or TV apps: a long channel list, a cramped guide and controls that were never designed for a phone. TivEPG was built in Swift for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV from day one, so it feels like an Apple app. It is fast to scroll, works with AirPlay and Picture in Picture, and keeps your playback going while you browse.

It is a player, not a provider. You add the playlist you already have and TivEPG builds your channels, guide, movies and series from it.

What you get on iPhone

  • A proper TV guide (EPG). Scroll the whole grid while the channel keeps playing in a mini-player, set reminders and jump into catch-up.
  • Xtream Codes and M3U. Log in with your provider's server, username and password, or paste an M3U playlist URL with an optional XMLTV guide URL.
  • Live TV, movies and series. Your provider's VOD library with artwork, synopses, Continue Watching and Trakt sync.
  • Catch-up and Replay. Watch back anything your channels keep in their archive, or press Replay to rewind to the last goal.
  • Multiview. Up to four channels on one screen. Pick which one you hear.
  • Sports Hub. Every fixture and live score for football, NFL, NBA, F1, cricket, tennis, golf and more, and the channel in your playlist that is showing it.
  • No spoilers. IPTV often runs a minute or two behind. TivEPG reads the match clock on your picture and holds score alerts until the goal happens on your screen.
  • Live Activities. Follow your team's score on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, even with the app closed.
  • Picture in Picture, AirPlay and Google Cast. Keep the game in the corner while you reply to messages, or send it to the TV.
  • Spoiler-free highlights. Scores stay hidden until you have watched the game.

How to add IPTV to your iPhone

  1. Download TivEPG from the App Store.
  2. Open Settings → Playlists → Add Playlist.
  3. Choose Xtream Codes (server URL, username, password) or M3U Playlist (playlist URL, plus a guide URL if your provider gives you one).
  4. TivEPG loads your channels and TV guide. Head to the Sports Hub to see which of your channels has today's games.

Setting it up for someone else? Use TivEPG Remote to do it from your browser. There is a full walkthrough in the set-up guide.

Is there TiviMate for iPhone?

No. TiviMate is Android only. If you are moving from TiviMate or IPTV Smarters to an iPhone, TivEPG gives you the same kind of full-screen guide plus things those apps do not have, like channel matching for live sport. See TivEPG as a TiviMate alternative.

Does TivEPG come with channels?

No. TivEPG is a player only. Add your own Xtream Codes login or M3U playlist from your provider.

Can I watch IPTV on iPhone with an M3U link?

Yes. In TivEPG open Settings, Playlists, Add Playlist, choose M3U Playlist and paste your playlist URL. Add an XMLTV guide URL if you have one.
